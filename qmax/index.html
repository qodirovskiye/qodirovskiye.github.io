03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more
clarity.

What does QMax actually analyze?+

QMax detects facial landmarks using Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker and calculates visible, two-dimensional proportions: face height-to-width, eye spacing, nose and mouth width, jaw width, and photo asymmetry. They describe this photo, not your value or attractiveness.

Is this the same service as QOVES?+

No. QMax is an independent, free experimental project inspired by facial-analysis services. It is not affiliated with QOVES and does not use their private models, reference datasets, human reviewers, or medical protocols.

Will it rate my attractiveness?+

You can optionally reveal a 1–10 experimental aesthetic score in “Score & skin care.” It uses an explained, rule-based geometric rubric. It is not a trained attractiveness model, professional evaluation, or population ranking; different aesthetic preferences would give different scores.

How accurate are the results?+

These are estimates from a single photo. Camera perspective, expression, lighting, hair, and head position affect the landmarks and ratios. Use consistent photos for comparison. Ratios are not physical millimeter measurements or clinical assessments.

Where are my photos and reports stored?+

They stay in memory unless you choose to save the report on this device. Saved reports and a reduced-size preview go into this browser's local database. Clearing website data removes them. Reports do not sync between devices.

Can QMax help with skin care?+

Yes. Choose the skin type and concerns you have noticed to get a simple AM/PM routine with links to American Academy of Dermatology guidance. These answers—not the facial scan—select the routine. Diagnoses, prescriptions, and procedure decisions need a qualified clinician.

Measurements,
with context.

QMax is a free, experimental tool for exploring visible facial geometry. Every result comes from the photo you supply.

How it works

Google MediaPipe Face Landmarker locates a mesh of 478 points. QMax converts their normalized coordinates to image pixels, compensates for in-plane head roll, and calculates two-dimensional ratios. No demographic reference populations or trained attractiveness model are used. An optional experimental aesthetic score applies an openly stated, hand-authored geometric rubric; it has not been calibrated against human attractiveness judgments.

What is measured

Visible face height versus cheek width, inner-eye spacing versus mean eye width, nose-to-mouth width, mouth-to-face width, jaw-to-cheek width, eye opening-to-width, midface-to-lower-face height, and a paired-landmark photo-asymmetry estimate. Face height begins at the upper face mesh boundary, not the hairline. Jaw points are surface landmarks, not bone measurements.

Why context matters

One photo is a perspective projection. Distance, lens, expression, head pose, occlusion, and model error change the result. QMax rejects multiple faces and strongly turned or tilted poses, and flags smaller issues. The quality rules are capture heuristics, not a validated accuracy score. Small differences between reports may be measurement noise.

What the suggestions mean

The exploration checklist is a transparent set of styling ideas based on your selected goal and measured ratios. The skin-care tab adds source-linked general routines based on self-reported skin concerns, with pregnancy and prescription-treatment cautions. These are not inferred diagnoses. It is not generated by a clinician or personal stylist and is not a treatment protocol. Measurements do not imply that a feature needs to change.

Privacy, in practice

The model, WebAssembly runtime, and example portrait are downloaded from this website. Your selected photo is processed locally in a dedicated worker. No analysis request or photo is sent to a server. Saved reports use IndexedDB in this browser. Hosting providers can still receive ordinary page and asset requests, including your IP address. There is no analytics, account system, or third-party script loaded from a CDN.
